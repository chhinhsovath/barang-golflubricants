Automechanika Frankfurt 2024

AUTOMECHANIKA FRANKFURT in Germany is the largest exhibition event in Europe and is aimed at customers from the automotive industry all over the world. The venue itself is an extremely interesting place, as Frankfurt is one of the most important financial metropolises in the world and a well-known exhibition center. At our stand you could find not only VENOL MOTOR OIL products, which enjoy world fame, but also a new brand – NEXIUM. NEXIUM – these are the most advanced engine oils, which raise the lubricating properties to a higher level. NEXIUM is the result of the extensive experience of our experts from engineering and laboratory. NEXIUM oils were developed for the most demanding automotive market, therefore our stand enjoyed great popularity among visitors. Thank you for the opportunity to present our VENOL MOTOR OIL products, as well as the new NEXIUM brand.
