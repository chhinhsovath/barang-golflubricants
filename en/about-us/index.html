About us

Established in 2002 as a limited liability company in Ladbergen, northern Germany, VENOL Motor Oil has always remained dedicated to creating top-quality lubricants and car care products, surpassing the expectations of our esteemed customers. To deliver on this promise, we proudly launched our products under the VENOL© brand in 2003.

Using modern technologies in 2004, we began our journey towards innovation, creating a new generation of products that not only matched but exceeded the requirements established by the major companies in the sector of motor oils, car care products and cosmetics.

In 2010–2014, we thoroughly refurbished our manufacturing plants to ensure compliance with the most stringent quality standards.
In 2019, VENOL© has undergone a complete rebranding and revamped and refreshed its product offering.

In 2022, we launched www.venolshop.com, a modern B2B platform designed to improve the transaction process and increase availability.
