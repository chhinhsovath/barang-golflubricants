Private label

We will create your brand

If you’re looking for a trusted partner to create a unique line of oils, lubricants, or automotive chemicals under your own brand, you’ve come to the right place.

At VENOL Motor Oil, we combine over 40 years of industry experience with a passion for creating products of the highest quality. We provide comprehensive support throughout the entire Private Label production process – from the very first drop to the finished, packaged product.

Why trust us?

State-of-the-art laboratory and R&D department

With our state-of-the-art laboratory facilities, we can develop and rigorously test formulations tailored to your specific technical and quality requirements.

In-house HDPE packaging production

Using our own state-of-the-art production lines, we manufacture packaging through extrusion blow moulding. We offer bottles ranging from 0.5 to 20 litres in your choice of colour, with the option to emboss your logo or company name.

The highest-quality components

All our products are made exclusively from premium, carefully selected raw materials sourced from leading international suppliers.
